## Delivery context and objective

MHCS is Admin-centered and Admin-first. An authorized Admin needs one coherent path to register or link an individual Radiographer or Radiologist, understand account and profession-specific eligibility, perform explicitly authorized lifecycle actions, and reach existing examination-scoped assignment actions. Account creation must never establish qualifications, clinical authority or an examination entitlement.

**Objective:** Admin → individual staff identity/account → independently authorized profession-specific eligibility → readiness for a specific assignment → existing Radiographer/Radiologist workspace. This is one bounded staff journey, not a generic identity-management platform. The complete journey is proposed for later execution only after the decisions below are approved.

## Scope

### In scope after authority closure and a later E6 handoff

- One minimized Admin staff directory/detail journey for individual Radiographers and Radiologists, with account state, profession-specific eligibility/scope and why a particular assignment is allowed or withheld.
- Register a new Shared User or link an existing individual only under D1–D3; preserve linked identity and account history without automatic person merges.
- Bounded account activation, suspension and recovery actions only as approved under D2/D7/D8. Distinguish account state, professional eligibility and examination assignment as separate controls.
- Operator-owned Radiographer eligibility and Doctor-owned Radiologist eligibility workflows under their independently approved verifier/facility permissions. Show pending/unverified state without claiming readiness.
- Reach existing worklist/assignment actions with server-derived facility/case/handoff context and current authorization. No entitlement merely from directory selection.
- Source, focused tests, owner contracts, fixtures, UI and additive schema changes necessary for this same journey may be proposed at finalization; their exact need follows approved decisions. Nothing is authorized to implement now.

### Out of scope

- Generic IAM, SSO/MFA/provider selection, HR/organization platform, self-service public signup, bulk import, automated licensing checks, scheduling/payroll/payout management or privilege editor.
- Patient migration, duplicate Patient resolution, Member registration redesign, account/portal retirement or conversion of staff into Members.
- Rebuilding assignments/viewers, changing Radiographer QC/protocol/durations, Doctor clinical judgment/reporting/finalization/durations, Member publication, AI behavior, source inventory or clinical-source bindings.
- Historical credential-exposure remediation, dependency/CI/runtime repair, G10 closure, production credentials, real staff/patient data and production operations.

### Preserved behavior

Preserve MVP-DEC-044–046/051 and each predecessor's approval scope; existing Member registration and identity/MRN/history; legacy Operator profile grants for their existing callers; independently granted multi-role access unless an explicit approved lifecycle action intentionally withdraws it; exact profession/facility/service/modality/case eligibility; technical handoff and clinical finalization/publication separation; original images/checksums/source lineage; append-only actor/audit history; signed-link and private-output protections. Staff management transfers no domain ownership to Admin.
